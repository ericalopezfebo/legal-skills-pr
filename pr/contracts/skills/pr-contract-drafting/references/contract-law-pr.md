# Puerto Rico contract-law drafting reference

## Baseline

Under Puerto Rico's 2020 Civil Code, contract drafting begins with the legal nature of the transaction rather than a universal form.

A contract may create, regulate, modify, or extinguish obligations. Party autonomy is not unlimited: contractual terms cannot contravene mandatory law, morality, or public order.

## Form

Do not assume all contracts must be written. Determine whether the specific transaction is subject to a statutory formality, including a private or public instrument, notarization, registration, or another special requirement.

Examples of transactions can implicate formal requirements concerning real rights in immovable property, certain leases, hereditary or marital rights, powers, and assignments connected to public instruments. Always verify the current text of the Código Civil before stating the precise requirement.

## Perfection and conditions

Identify whether the agreement is perfected by consent or whether a solemn form or suspensive condition affects effectiveness.

## Adhesion

If terms are pre-drafted and the accepting party has no meaningful opportunity to negotiate, identify the possibility that the agreement or provision may be treated as adhesion language. Do not use the label as a substitute for the governing legal analysis.

## Special regimes

Before relying solely on the Civil Code, screen for:
- consumer law;
- employment law;
- government/public contracting;
- real property;
- intellectual property;
- corporate authority;
- federal preemption or overlay;
- regulated industries.

## Drafting principle

The clause map must follow the transaction and governing law. Never import a clause merely because it is customary in a U.S. template.
