# Research leads and provenance

These sources are research leads, not a substitute for primary authority.

## Secondary orientation
- Ayuda Legal Puerto Rico — “¿Qué es un contrato?”: overview of contract validity, form, perfection, preliminary agreements and adhesion contracts.
- Microjuris al Día — “¿Cómo mejorar mi redacción jurídica?”: clarity, organization, precision, citations, tone, active voice and editing.
- Microjuris al Día — commentary on contract form and adhesion contracts.
- Microjuris al Día — coverage of non-compete clauses and recent Puerto Rico Supreme Court developments involving assignment, options, regulated contracts, public contracting and labor law.

## Required research behavior
When a secondary source identifies a statute, regulation or Puerto Rico Supreme Court case:
1. locate the primary source;
2. verify current validity;
3. cite the primary source for the legal proposition;
4. use the secondary source only for context or as a research lead.

Never transform a news headline or commentary into a holding without reading the underlying authority.
