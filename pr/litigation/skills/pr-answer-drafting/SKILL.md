# Puerto Rico Answer Drafting

## Purpose
Prepare a responsive pleading that tracks the complaint and preserves only defenses supported by the record and current law.

## Workflow
1. Identify service date, response deadline, court and procedural posture.
2. Preserve the complaint's paragraph numbering.
3. For each allegation classify the response: admit, deny, admit in part/deny in part, or lack sufficient knowledge where procedurally proper.
4. Never deny an allegation known to be true merely for strategy.
5. Identify defenses potentially applicable to the supplied facts.
6. Verify the legal basis and current status of each affirmative defense before including it.
7. Identify compulsory/permissive counterclaim issues when relevant; do not invent a counterclaim.
8. Draft the requested relief and any required filing sections.
9. Run a consistency audit against the complaint.

## Defense rule
Do not dump a boilerplate list of affirmative defenses. Include a defense only if there is a reasonable factual/legal basis, or clearly flag it for attorney verification where the record is incomplete.

## Output
A finished answer should mirror the complaint closely enough that each response is traceable to the allegation answered.
